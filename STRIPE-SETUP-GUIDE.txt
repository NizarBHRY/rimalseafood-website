RIMAL SEAFOOD — ACCEPTING ONLINE CARD PAYMENTS (STRIPE)
=========================================================
This is optional. Without it, "Place Order" still works exactly as
before — customers pay at pickup or in person. This guide adds a
second button, "Pay Online with Card", so customers can pay by card
right on the website before they arrive.

Do SETUP-GUIDE.txt first if you haven't already — this depends on
the database from that guide already being connected.

Total time: about 15 minutes. Stripe has no monthly fee — you're
only charged when a payment goes through: 2.9% + 30 cents per
transaction (standard rate, same as most restaurants pay).


STEP 1 — CREATE YOUR STRIPE ACCOUNT
-------------------------------------
  1. Go to stripe.com and click "Start now" / "Sign up"
  2. Enter your business info (Rimal Seafood, your address, your bank
     account for payouts). Stripe deposits your money to your bank
     automatically, usually within 2 business days of each payment.
  3. You can finish this later — Stripe lets you test everything in
     "Test mode" first, before your account is fully approved.


STEP 2 — ADD THE DATABASE COLUMN
-----------------------------------
  1. In Supabase, click "SQL Editor" > "New query"
  2. Open stripe-setup.sql (in this folder) in Notepad, copy all of it
  3. Paste into the SQL Editor, click "Run"

This adds one column so the database can tell which orders were
paid online vs. paid at pickup.


STEP 3 — GET YOUR STRIPE TEST KEYS
-------------------------------------
  1. In your Stripe Dashboard, make sure the toggle in the top right
     says "Test mode" (this lets you try everything with fake cards
     before using real money)
  2. Click "Developers" > "API keys"
  3. Copy the "Secret key" (starts with sk_test_...) — you'll paste
     this into Netlify in Step 4, NEVER into any website file.


STEP 4 — ADD YOUR KEY TO NETLIFY
------------------------------------
  1. Go to app.netlify.com, click into your Rimal Seafood site
  2. Go to: Site configuration > Environment variables
  3. Click "Add a variable" and add these one at a time:

       STRIPE_SECRET_KEY            = (the sk_test_... key from Step 3)
       SUPABASE_URL                 = (same Project URL from config.js)
       SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY    = (see note below)

     For SUPABASE_SERVICE_ROLE_KEY: in Supabase, go to Project
     Settings > API, and copy the "service_role" "secret" key — the
     one you were told NEVER to put in config.js. This is the one
     place it's safe to use it, because Netlify environment variables
     are never visible to website visitors, only to your own server
     functions.

  4. Click "Deploy" > "Trigger deploy" > "Deploy site" so Netlify
     picks up the new variables.


STEP 5 — CONNECT THE WEBHOOK (so payments always get recorded)
-------------------------------------------------------------------
This step tells Stripe to notify your site the instant a payment
succeeds, even if the customer closes their browser right after
paying.

  1. In Stripe Dashboard (still in Test mode), go to:
     Developers > Webhooks > "Add endpoint"
  2. Endpoint URL:  https://YOUR-NETLIFY-SITE.netlify.app/.netlify/functions/stripe-webhook
     (replace YOUR-NETLIFY-SITE with your actual Netlify address, or
     your real domain once it's connected)
  3. Click "Select events", choose "checkout.session.completed",
     click "Add events"
  4. Click "Add endpoint"
  5. Click into the endpoint you just made, find "Signing secret",
     click "Reveal", copy it (starts with whsec_...)
  6. Back in Netlify > Environment variables, add:

       STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET = (the whsec_... value)

  7. Trigger another deploy in Netlify so it picks this up too.


STEP 6 — TEST IT (still in Test mode — no real money moves)
----------------------------------------------------------------
  1. On your live Netlify site, add something to the cart
  2. Click "Pay Online with Card"
  3. On the Stripe checkout page, use this test card:
       Card number: 4242 4242 4242 4242
       Expiry: any future date       CVC: any 3 digits
       ZIP: any 5 digits
  4. Complete payment — you should land back on the site with a
     "Payment received" message
  5. In Supabase, open the "orders" table, find that order, confirm
     its "payment_status" column now says "paid"


STEP 7 — GO LIVE (real payments)
-------------------------------------
  1. Finish your Stripe account setup (business details, bank
     account) if you haven't already
  2. In Stripe, switch the toggle from "Test mode" to live, go to
     Developers > API keys again, copy the LIVE secret key
     (starts with sk_live_...)
  3. Repeat Step 5 once more in live mode (webhooks are separate
     between test and live) to get a live whsec_... secret
  4. In Netlify, update STRIPE_SECRET_KEY and STRIPE_WEBHOOK_SECRET
     with the live values from steps 2-3
  5. Trigger a deploy. Real cards will now be charged for real.


WHAT CUSTOMERS SEE
----------------------
"Place Order" (pay at pickup) and "Pay Online with Card" (pay now)
both appear in the cart. Either way, the order goes to your Manager
Dashboard the same way. Orders paid online are marked "paid" —
everything else defaults to "unpaid" since that just means "not
paid through the website yet," not that anything is wrong.
