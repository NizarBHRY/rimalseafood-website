Our Story

A Table by the Water, In Spirit

Rimal Seafood is a brand-new arrival to Brooklyn, opening its doors in 2026 with one belief: seafood should taste like the sea it came from — whether it's plated at our table or wrapped up from our fish market case.

Every fillet, shell, and claw is sourced within 24 hours of landing. Our kitchen handles it with a light touch for the dining room, and our market counter cleans, scales, and cuts it fresh to order for cooking at home.
