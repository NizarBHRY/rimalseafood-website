RIMAL SEAFOOD — HOW TO PUT THIS SITE ON rimalseafood.com
=========================================================

DO SETUP-GUIDE.txt FIRST
--------------------------
Before deploying, read and follow SETUP-GUIDE.txt in this same folder.
It connects the site to a real database so that orders placed on a
customer's own phone actually reach your Manager Dashboard. Skipping
it means the site will look right but "Place Order" will show an
error, since there's nowhere for the order to go yet.

Once SETUP-GUIDE.txt is done (config.js has your real database keys
pasted in), come back here.


WHAT'S IN THIS FOLDER
---------------------
  index.html            <- the whole website (this is the main file)
  rimal-logo.jpg        <- your logo (also used as the browser tab icon)
  config.js             <- your database connection keys (from setup guide)
  supabase-setup.sql     <- one-time database setup script (from setup guide)
  SETUP-GUIDE.txt        <- online ordering setup (do this first)
  netlify.toml            <- tells Netlify where to find the files below
  netlify/functions/      <- powers online card payments (optional)
  stripe-setup.sql        <- one-time database update for online payments
  STRIPE-SETUP-GUIDE.txt  <- online card payment setup (optional, do after online ordering works)
  delivery-setup.sql      <- one-time database update for the Delivery option

Keep ALL of these files together in the same folder, including the
netlify folder — don't rename or remove anything.


STEP 1 — BUY THE DOMAIN (about 5 minutes, ~$12/year)
----------------------------------------------------
Go to any of these and search for "rimalseafood.com":
  - namecheap.com        (simple, good prices)
  - cloudflare.com/products/registrar   (cheapest, no markup)
  - godaddy.com          (most common, watch for upsells)

Buy it. Decline the extra add-ons they try to sell you
(website builder, email, SEO packages) — you don't need them.


STEP 2 — CREATE FREE HOSTING (about 5 minutes, $0)
---------------------------------------------------
Recommended: NETLIFY (easiest — no technical setup)

  1. Go to netlify.com and click "Sign up" (free account)
  2. Once logged in, find the box that says
     "Drag and drop your site output folder here"
  3. Drag THIS ENTIRE FOLDER (rimalseafood-website) into that box
  4. Wait ~20 seconds. Netlify gives you a temporary address like
     random-name-12345.netlify.app — click it and confirm the site works.

Your site is now live on the internet (just not on your domain yet).


STEP 3 — CONNECT YOUR DOMAIN (about 5 minutes)
-----------------------------------------------
  1. In Netlify, go to:  Site settings > Domain management > Add domain
  2. Type: rimalseafood.com  and click Verify / Add
  3. Netlify will show you DNS records (usually nameservers, or an A record
     pointing to 75.2.60.5 plus a CNAME for "www")
  4. Log in to where you bought the domain (Step 1)
     Find "DNS settings" or "Nameservers"
     Enter exactly what Netlify told you
  5. Save. Wait 10 minutes to a few hours for it to take effect.

HTTPS (the padlock icon) turns on automatically once the domain connects.
You don't have to buy an SSL certificate.


STEP 4 — UPDATING THE SITE LATER
---------------------------------
Any time the menu or prices change:
  - Get the updated index.html
  - In Netlify, go to your site > Deploys tab
  - Drag the folder in again. It replaces the old version instantly.
  - Your domain and QR codes keep working — nothing else to change.

config.js and supabase-setup.sql don't need to be touched again once
Step 1–4 of SETUP-GUIDE.txt is done — your database keeps running on
Supabase's servers independently of Netlify.


HOW ORDERING WORKS NOW
-------------------------
Once SETUP-GUIDE.txt is complete, orders placed from ANY device —
a customer's phone at home, a tablet at the counter, your own laptop —
are saved to one shared database and appear in the Manager Dashboard
on every signed-in device within a couple of seconds. Rewards balances,
order history, and fish market stock levels are shared the same way.

The Manager Dashboard requires a real email + password login (created
in Supabase, see SETUP-GUIDE.txt Step 4) instead of the old 4-digit PIN,
since it now shows real customer names and phone numbers.
